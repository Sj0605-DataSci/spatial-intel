**Rules for Hospital Heroes:**

1. **Patient Treatment:** Prioritize based on *Patient Health Level*. Urgent cases require immediate intervention to prevent health deterioration.

2. **Supply Usage:** Use *Resource Inventory* wisely. Overuse depletes supplies, affecting treatment capability.

3. **Staff Rotation:** *Staff Energy* depletes with tasks. Rotate staff to maintain efficiency and prevent burnout.

4. **Surgery Outcomes:** Dr. Samantha's *Operation Success Rate* affects patient recovery. Improve with successful surgeries.

5. **Maintain Cleanliness:** Ensure high *Cleanliness Level* to reduce infection risks and improve overall *Patient Satisfaction*.

6. **Emergency Management:** Respond to *Emergency Alerts* promptly to boost hospital reputation and patient outcomes.

7. **Resource Distribution:** Optimize *Supply Chain Efficiency* to ensure timely delivery to all wards.

**End Goal:** Game evaluates performance on patient recovery rates, hospital reputation, and effective resource management.
