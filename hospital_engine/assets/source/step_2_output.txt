**State Variables for Hospital Heroes:**

1. **Patient Health Level:** Indicates each patient's health status. Drives urgency and priority in care.

2. **Resource Inventory:** Tracks available medical supplies. Critical for strategic planning and decision-making.

3. **Staff Energy:** Measures the fatigue level of each character. Impacts performance and requires rest periods.

4. **Operation Success Rate:** Reflects Dr. Samantha Lee's effectiveness in surgeries, influencing patient outcomes.

5. **Patient Satisfaction:** Gauges overall patient morale and happiness, affecting hospital reputation and rewards.

6. **Cleanliness Level:** Managed by Janitor Malik, affects infection rates and impacts patient health recovery.

7. **Emergency Alert:** Signals priority cases needing immediate attention, escalating gameplay tension.

8. **Supply Chain Efficiency:** Managed by Ellen, tracks distribution speed of resources across wards.

**Game Over:** Outcomes vary based on patient recovery rates, overall reputation, and resource management success.
