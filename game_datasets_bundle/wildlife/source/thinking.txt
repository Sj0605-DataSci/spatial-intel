Title: Wildlife Rescue Odyssey

Overview:  
Players venture into diverse ecosystems to save endangered species. Through grid-based puzzles, they rebuild habitats and guide animals back to safety, uncovering tracks and tools. Each chapter tells a story of specific conservation challenges and successes, focusing on community efforts and animal behavior. The narrative expands with each puzzle completed, emphasizing the importance of biodiversity and understanding the unique role of each species. Although lacking audio and visual effects, impactful text narratives and evocative imagery aid in immersing players, engaging them emotionally with poignant stories of survival and hope in wildlife conservation.


--- STEP 1 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Characters:

1. **Conservationist Alex** – The main character, a passionate wildlife conservationist guiding players through missions, explaining the significance of each ecosystem and species.

2. **Orphaned Animal Guardians** – Animal companions like a curious elephant calf or a playful otter aiding players in locating hidden objects and navigating puzzles.

3. **Endangered Animals** – Species like tigers, pandas, and turtles symbolizing conservation targets. Rescuing them unlocks new puzzles and narratives about their importance.

4. **Local Villagers** – Community members provide insights and backstory, representing collective efforts in habitat restoration and wildlife protection.

Environment:  
Set across various ecosystems including rainforests, savannahs, and oceans, each with its unique flora and fauna, visually illustrating the biodiversity and conservation challenges of each region.


--- STEP 2 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Game States:

1. **Animals Rescued:** Tracks the number of endangered species saved, unlocking new puzzles and narrative segments.

2. **Habitat Restored:** Measures progress in rebuilding ecosystems, impacting the availability of future puzzle challenges.

3. **Conservation Points:** Earned through successful puzzle completions, these points reflect the player's conservation impact and influence story outcomes.

4. **Companion Skills:** Represents the abilities of animal companions, enhancing their effectiveness in solving puzzles and finding hidden items.

5. **Community Support:** Reflects the level of local villagers' involvement in conservation efforts, affecting available resources and hints.

6. **Environmental Health:** Indicates the current status of the ecosystem, influencing the complexity of puzzles and storyline progression.


--- STEP 3 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Rules:

1. **Rescue Mission:** Each endangered animal requires solving specific puzzles to be rescued, increasing "Animals Rescued."

2. **Ecosystem Restoration:** Completing habitat puzzles boosts "Habitat Restored," unlocking new regions and more complex challenges.

3. **Conservation Actions:** Gathering hidden items and completing tasks earns "Conservation Points," influencing the storyline.

4. **Companion Utilization:** Use animal companions' abilities strategically to find hidden objects or bypass obstacles, altering "Companion Skills."

5. **Community Engagement:** Interact with villagers to gain insights or solve puzzles, enhancing "Community Support" and available resources.

6. **Ecosystem Health:** Actions impact "Environmental Health," affecting puzzle difficulty and narrative paths.

End Goal: Rescuing all animals and fully restoring habitats unlocks a final narrative celebrating conservation efforts and ecosystem revival.
