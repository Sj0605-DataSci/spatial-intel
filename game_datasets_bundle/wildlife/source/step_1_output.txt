Characters:

1. **Conservationist Alex** – The main character, a passionate wildlife conservationist guiding players through missions, explaining the significance of each ecosystem and species.

2. **Orphaned Animal Guardians** – Animal companions like a curious elephant calf or a playful otter aiding players in locating hidden objects and navigating puzzles.

3. **Endangered Animals** – Species like tigers, pandas, and turtles symbolizing conservation targets. Rescuing them unlocks new puzzles and narratives about their importance.

4. **Local Villagers** – Community members provide insights and backstory, representing collective efforts in habitat restoration and wildlife protection.

Environment:  
Set across various ecosystems including rainforests, savannahs, and oceans, each with its unique flora and fauna, visually illustrating the biodiversity and conservation challenges of each region.
