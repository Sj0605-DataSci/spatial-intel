### Game Summary

**Game Title:** Diwali Festival Adventure

**Game Outline:**
Players step into the shoes of Arjun, actively preparing for a Diwali celebration. The game integrates interactive tasks such as lighting diyas, preparing meals, wrapping gifts, and organizing prayers. Players also engage dynamically with family and community events, enhancing the cultural immersion and theme depth.

### Characters

1. **Arjun**: The enthusiastic protagonist leading festive preparations.
2. **Grandmother Meera**: Offers guidance and shares stories, enriching cultural context.
3. **Uncle Ravi**: Coordinates interactive community events, sharing Diwali history.
4. **Cousin Anya**: Infuses creativity into decorations and art.
5. **Neighbors**: Different community figures offer unique tasks, enhancing theme variety.

### State Variables

1. **Tasks Completed**: Tracks the completion of festive tasks.
2. **Joy Level**: Measures cumulative happiness, influencing event unlocks.
3. **Traditions Fulfilled**: Tracks adherence to cultural practices, affecting blessings.
4. **Creativity Meter**: Reflects decorative creativity, boosting joy and event variety.
5. **Community Connection**: Gauges engagement with community, unlocking community festivals.

### Rules

1. **Task Completion**: Completing tasks progresses the story and unlocks interactions.
2. **Joy Increase**: Success in tasks elevates joy, unlocking special scenes and rewards.
3. **Traditional Accuracy**: Properly executed tasks under guidance enhance blessings and unlock traditional stories.
4. **Creative Adds**: Collaborate on decor to boost creativity and reveal unique content.
5. **Community Bonds**: Engaging with neighbors strengthens connections and triggers community events.

### Enhanced Mechanics

1. **Interactive Community Events**: Participate in dynamic events like lantern-lighting ceremonies or group prayers. These events adapt based on "Community Connection" levels, deepening immersion.

2. **Personalized Family Interactions**: Choices in task execution affect family dynamics and individual character story arcs, increasing emotional impact.

3. **Dynamic Environment**: The neighborhood evolves based on player actions; for example, streets light up more vibrantly with higher "Joy Levels."
