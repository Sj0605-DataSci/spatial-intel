Characters:

1. **Eli, the Eco-Warrior**: A determined young environmentalist, leads the city restoration. Guides players and boosts morale by sharing stories of hope and transformation.

2. **Mr. Green, the Shopkeeper**: Struggles with waste management in his store. Represents everyday challenges and emphasizes learning sustainable practices. Offers rewards for successful recycling projects.

3. **Ms. Meadows, the Teacher**: Inspires students and players with knowledge on recycling's benefits. Challenges players with quizzes that enhance understanding and gameplay strategy.

4. **Tara, the Gardener**: Transformed vacant lots into community gardens. Represents resourcefulness and creativity. Provides players with seeds and tools for green projects.

5. **Mayor Bright**: Overseeing city revival efforts, challenges players to complete city-wide recycling goals. Represents community leadership and collective effort.

6. **Junkbots**: Playful, recycled robots assist in sorting and upcycling tasks. Embody the spirit of innovation and reuse.
