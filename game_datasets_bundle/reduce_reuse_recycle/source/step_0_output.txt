Title: Green Guardians: City Revival

Overview: Set in a city plagued by pollution, players become eco-heroes tasked with restoring balance. Combine decoration and shape-fitting gameplay by sorting recyclables into matching bins and crafting community installations on a grid. Each level reveals heartfelt stories of residents whose lives transform through collective recycling efforts. Characters face challenges: a shopkeeper struggling with waste, a teacher inspiring students through green projects. As players progress, they witness visible environmental changes and emotional breakthroughs, deepening investment in themes of reduction, reuse, and recycling. Join events that simulate real-world recycling milestones, reinforcing community bonds and personal stakes.
