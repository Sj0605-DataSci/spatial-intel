Title: Green Guardians: City Revival

Overview: Set in a city plagued by pollution, players become eco-heroes tasked with restoring balance. Combine decoration and shape-fitting gameplay by sorting recyclables into matching bins and crafting community installations on a grid. Each level reveals heartfelt stories of residents whose lives transform through collective recycling efforts. Characters face challenges: a shopkeeper struggling with waste, a teacher inspiring students through green projects. As players progress, they witness visible environmental changes and emotional breakthroughs, deepening investment in themes of reduction, reuse, and recycling. Join events that simulate real-world recycling milestones, reinforcing community bonds and personal stakes.


--- STEP 1 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Characters:

1. **Eli, the Eco-Warrior**: A determined young environmentalist, leads the city restoration. Guides players and boosts morale by sharing stories of hope and transformation.

2. **Mr. Green, the Shopkeeper**: Struggles with waste management in his store. Represents everyday challenges and emphasizes learning sustainable practices. Offers rewards for successful recycling projects.

3. **Ms. Meadows, the Teacher**: Inspires students and players with knowledge on recycling's benefits. Challenges players with quizzes that enhance understanding and gameplay strategy.

4. **Tara, the Gardener**: Transformed vacant lots into community gardens. Represents resourcefulness and creativity. Provides players with seeds and tools for green projects.

5. **Mayor Bright**: Overseeing city revival efforts, challenges players to complete city-wide recycling goals. Represents community leadership and collective effort.

6. **Junkbots**: Playful, recycled robots assist in sorting and upcycling tasks. Embody the spirit of innovation and reuse.


--- STEP 2 FINAL OUTPUT ---
State Variables:

1. **Recyclables Sorted**: Tracks materials correctly placed in bins. Drives progression and unlocks new areas of the city.

2. **Community Projects Completed**: Measures upcycled installations done by players, impacting environmental transformation and unlocking character narratives.

3. **Character Satisfaction**: Reflects happiness levels of characters (e.g., Mr. Green, Ms. Meadows) as players meet recycling challenges, influencing story outcomes.

4. **Progress Meter**: Indicates overall city revival progress. Affects player morale and visual changes in the environment.

5. **Eco-Credits**: Earned by completing tasks, used to purchase seeds, tools, and unlock special events with Tara and Eli.

6. **Knowledge Points**: Gained from Ms. Meadows' quizzes, used to unlock educational tips and resources within the game.

7. **Junkbot Efficiency**: Monitors the effectiveness of Junkbots in assisting tasks, directly affecting how quickly players can complete sorting and upcycling activities.


--- STEP 3 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Rules:

1. **Sorting Rule**: Drag and drop recyclables into correct bins to increase "Recyclables Sorted." Incorrect placement resets progress in that grid area.

2. **Project Completion**: Use collected recyclables to complete "Community Projects." When finished, unlock character narratives and increase "Character Satisfaction."

3. **Character Interaction**: Complete tasks requested by characters to raise "Character Satisfaction." Higher satisfaction unlocks special abilities and rewards.

4. **Eco-Credits Rule**: Earn "Eco-Credits" by sorting and completing projects. Spend them to purchase items for new community projects.

5. **Knowledge Quizzes**: Answer Ms. Meadows' quizzes correctly to earn "Knowledge Points." Use points to unlock educational resources for better gameplay strategies.

6. **Junkbot Management**: Maintain "Junkbot Efficiency" by regularly upgrading bots. Efficient Junkbots aid in faster sorting and upcycling.

7. **Progress Achievement**: Measure "Progress Meter" via tasks completed. Full progress transforms the city, signaling ecological victory and ends the game with a celebratory message.
