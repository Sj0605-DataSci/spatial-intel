**Game Summary:**

**Title:** Green Guardians: City Revival

**Overview:** Players are tasked with transforming a polluted city into an eco-friendly community. Combining decoration, shape-fitting, and strategic decision-making, the game revolves around the themes of reduce, reuse, and recycle. Players sort recyclables, complete community projects, and interact with local characters, enhancing their understanding of environmental conservation.

**Characters:**

1. **Eli, the Eco-Warrior:** Guides players with strategic advice and motivation.
2. **Mr. Green, the Shopkeeper:** Faces real-world waste management challenges, seeking efficiency.
3. **Ms. Meadows, the Teacher:** Delivers insights on sustainability and eco-friendly practices.
4. **Tara, the Gardener:** Facilitates greening initiatives with upcycling tips.
5. **Mayor Bright:** Sets recycling goals and emphasizes sustainable leadership.
6. **Junkbots:** Assist with energy-efficient recycling and cleanup tasks.

**State Variables:**

1. **Recyclables Sorted:** Tracks correctly sorted items.
2. **Community Projects Completed:** Measures upcycled installations.
3. **Character Satisfaction:** Reflects happiness and motivation levels.
4. **Progress Meter:** Indicates city revival and transformation status.
5. **Eco-Credits:** Earned for upgrading tools, engaging in renewable energy initiatives, and purchasing resources.
6. **Knowledge Points:** Gained from quizzes for unlocking sustainable technologies.
7. **Junkbot Efficiency:** Monitors task efficiency and reduces energy usage.

**Rules:**

1. Correctly sort recyclables to enhance "Recyclables Sorted" and earn rewards.
2. Use sorted materials to complete "Community Projects," unlocking character interactions and bonuses.
3. Increase "Character Satisfaction" by fulfilling requests, unlocking strategic benefits.
4. Spend "Eco-Credits" on renewable energy projects and eco-upgrades.
5. Answer quizzes for "Knowledge Points," which unlock green technologies.
6. Maintain "Junkbot Efficiency" to optimize tasks and minimize energy consumption.
7. Achieve full "Progress Meter" through strategic decisions, transforming the city and achieving an eco-friendly victory.

**Enhanced Mechanics:**

- **Energy Management:** Introduces mechanics related to solar and wind energy deployment.
- **Pollution Reduction Challenges:** Engage players in initiatives to lower pollution levels, earning bonus points and enhancing the city's visual transformation.

**Image Prompt Description:**

- **Eli, the Eco-Warrior:** Energetic and resourceful, with eco-themed gadgets and attire that highlight his leadership in sustainability.
- **Mr. Green, the Shopkeeper:** Represents urban commerce adapting to green practices, with his shop showcasing energy-efficient lighting and waste reduction technologies.
- **Ms. Meadows, the Teacher:** Stands in an eco-laboratory, surrounded by green tech, illustrating the educational drive for sustainability.
- **Tara, the Gardener:** Shows innovation in urban farming, with upcycled structures and renewable watering systems.
- **Mayor Bright:** Depicts a visionary leader promoting community-driven solar panel installations and sustainable urban planning.
- **Junkbots:** Feature renewable energy adapters and recycling enhancements, manifesting the theme of eco-efficiency.

- **Environment:** Illustrates a dynamic shift from pollution to green urban spaces, featuring solar panels on rooftops, community gardens, and depollution efforts, visually representing the city's sustainable evolution.
