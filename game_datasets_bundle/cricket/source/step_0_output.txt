**Game Title:** Cricket Battle Grid

**Genre:** Memory & Sequence Solving

**Overview:**  
Enter the climactic moments of a cricket match on a grid. Players act as team captains tasked with crafting a last-over victory. Memorize opposing field arrangements and player stats to strategize effectively. Set your batting order and line-up sequences to outmaneuver the opposing team. Experience the pressure of needing 20 runs off the final over; position your players strategically to hit sixes or find gaps in the field. Overcome dilemmas like choosing between a risky shot or a safe single. Each play evokes the tension and thrill of cricket, immersing you in the emotional highs and lows of this beloved sport.
