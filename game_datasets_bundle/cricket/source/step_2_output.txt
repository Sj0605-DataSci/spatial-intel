**State Variables:**

1. **Runs Required**  
   Tracks remaining runs needed for victory. Reflects match pressure, influencing strategic decisions by the Captain Strategist.

2. **Wickets in Hand**  
   Monitors remaining batsmen, impacting the risk level Ace Batsman can take. Critical for tactical planning.

3. **Overs Remaining**  
   Shows time left in the match, pressing the Spin Wizard to craft effective bowling scenarios.

4. **Player Morale**  
   Affects performance. Influenced by successful plays or setbacks, triggers character growth, especially for the Rookie Player.

5. **Field Arrangement**  
   Visual representation of field strategy. Changes with Captain Strategist’s decisions, affecting Run Requirement.

6. **Player Stamina**  
   Influences effectiveness. Impacts the Field Master’s agility and Ace Batsman’s shot accuracy, requiring strategic substitutions.

7. **Partnership Score**  
   Tracks key run partnerships, enhancing team dynamics and relationships, emphasizing collaborative success.
