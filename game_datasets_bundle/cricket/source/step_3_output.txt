**Rules:**

1. **Run Chase**  
   Players must achieve 'Runs Required' before 'Overs Remaining' count reaches zero for victory.

2. **Batting Strategy**  
   Ace Batsman decides risk level: higher risks can decrease 'Runs Required' faster, but increase chances of losing 'Wickets in Hand'.

3. **Field Setup**  
   Modify 'Field Arrangement' to reduce opponent scoring or intercept runs. Strategic positioning boosts 'Player Morale'.

4. **Bowling Plan**  
   Spin Wizard alters bowling style to manipulate 'Player Morale' and 'Partnership Score' of opponents.

5. **Stamina Management**  
   Monitor 'Player Stamina'; exhaustion prompts substitution or position change for optimal performance.

6. **Morale Impact**  
   Success increases 'Player Morale', enhancing skills; low morale hinders performance, especially for the Rookie.

7. **Partnership Building**  
   Enhance 'Partnership Score' by sustaining batting pairs to increase run potential and morale.

**End Game:**  
Achieve the target score with 'Wickets in Hand' before 'Overs Remaining' runs out. Celebrate victory with thematic highlights.
