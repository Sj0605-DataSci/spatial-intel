**Characters:**

1. **The Curator:**  
   The player's guide, offering historical insights and dilemmas about preservation vs. development. Embodies wisdom and knowledge.

2. **The Architect Spirits:**  
   Spiritual guides of each wonder, representing the original architects. Provide clues and puzzles to unlock the wonder's story.

3. **Cultural Guardians:**  
   Embody the cultural heritage of each wonder. Tasked with protecting artifacts and sharing stories through hidden object challenges.

4. **Preservation Advocate:**  
   A character pushing for the protection of wonders, presenting dilemmas and conflicts regarding modernization impacts.

5. **Modern Developer:**  
   Represents the advancement vs. preservation conflict, introducing challenges that impact the player's choices.

6. **Artifacts:**  
   Symbolic representations of each wonder's history, requiring discovery to progress and unlock deeper narratives.

**Environment:**  
The game spans diverse landscapes where each wonder is set, providing a backdrop that emphasizes historical significance and evokes emotional connection to the past.
