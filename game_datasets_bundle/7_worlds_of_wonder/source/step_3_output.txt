**Rules:**

1. **Puzzle Completion:**  
   Solve puzzles to increase Wonder Restoration Progress, unlocking narratives with Architect Spirits' insights.

2. **Artifact Discovery:**  
   Find artifacts to boost Cultural Insight Level and Artifact Discovery Count, revealing historical stories from Cultural Guardians.

3. **Decision Making:**  
   Make choices affecting Preservation vs. Development Balance, influencing the narrative guided by Preservation Advocate and Modern Developer.

4. **Emotional Engagement:**  
   Engage with dilemmas to raise Emotional Connection Scale, affecting dialogue depth with the Curator and narrative progression.

5. **Puzzle Timeliness:**  
   Solve Time Anomalies promptly to maintain steady Wonder Restoration Progress, unlocking further narratives.

6. **Win Condition:**  
   Fully restore all wonders by maximizing Wonder Restoration Progress and Cultural Insight Level. Celebrate the wonders' preserved legacies.

These rules integrate gameplay mechanics with the narrative depth, making player actions meaningful in exploring the heritage and conservation challenges of the wonders.
