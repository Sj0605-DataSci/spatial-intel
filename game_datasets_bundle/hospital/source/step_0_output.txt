**Game Overview: Hospital Heroes**

**Genre:** Collection & Sorting

**Objective:** Navigate the intricacies of hospital management by ensuring optimal patient care and staff coordination.

**Gameplay:**

- **Collection:** Gather essential supplies like medications, surgical tools, and life-support equipment to prep for diverse patient cases.

- **Sorting:** Prioritize and triage patients based on condition severity. Assign them to appropriate rooms and allocate nurses and doctors for necessary treatments.

**Grid Setup:** The hospital grid includes various wards, operating rooms, and specialist stations. Players must efficiently manage the flow of patients and resources, reflecting real-world hospital pressures.

**Story Elements:**

- **Narrative Depth:** Each level presents unique storylines with distinct patient cases, such as a child needing emergency surgery or an elderly patient with chronic issues. Players experience the journey of hope, recovery, and occasional setbacks.

- **Character Development:** Engage with a diverse cast of characters, including compassionate nurses, diligent doctors, and anxious patients. Build relationships, understand their backstories, and face moral dilemmas like allocating scarce resources during a crisis.

- **Emotional Engagement:** Navigate emotionally charged scenarios that evoke empathy and understanding, such as comforting a distressed patient or celebrating successful recoveries. Experience the relentless dedication of hospital heroes working in a high-stakes environment.
