**Game Summary: Hospital Heroes**

**Game Outline:**  
"Hospital Heroes" immerses players in the role of hospital administrators tasked with managing a bustling medical facility. Players focus on optimizing patient care, resource management, and staff coordination to overcome daily challenges and critical emergencies.

**Characters:**

1. **Dr. Samantha Lee:** Veteran emergency surgeon, pivotal for high-stakes procedures and mentorship of junior staff.
2. **Nurse Javier Torres:** Compassionate caregiver, essential for patient morale and emotional support.
3. **Patient Lydia Bennett:** Represents various patient stories, enriching player empathy and engagement.
4. **Janitor Malik:** Ensures a germ-free environment, vital for patient health and well-being.
5. **Resource Manager Ellen:** Manages logistics and supply chains, ensuring resources meet operational demands.
6. **Resident Dr. Arun Patel:** A burgeoning doctor, reflecting growth and adaptability in challenging situations.

**State Variables:**

1. Patient Health Level
2. Resource Inventory
3. Staff Energy
4. Operation Success Rate
5. Patient Satisfaction
6. Cleanliness Level
7. Emergency Alert
8. Supply Chain Efficiency
9. Visitor Satisfaction
10. Medication Accuracy

**Game Rules:**

1. **Patient Care:** Interact and prioritize based on health status and emergencies for effective treatment plans.
2. **Resource Allocation:** Make strategic decisions to prevent shortages, crucial during peak times.
3. **Staff Management:** Rotate staff to maintain efficiency; encourage skill development and morale.
4. **Surgery Success:** Positive outcomes increase operation success rates, enhancing hospital reputation.
5. **Maintain Cleanliness:** Regularly scheduled cleaning prevents infections and boosts patient satisfaction.
6. **Emergency Response:** Rapid response to emergencies reflects hospital efficiency and reputation.
7. **Supply Chain Management:** Optimize distribution processes to ensure timely resource availability.
8. **Visitor Engagement:** Facilitate interactions, impacting patient morale and family satisfaction.
9. **Medication Management:** Dispense accurate treatments to maximize recovery potential.

**Image Prompt Descriptions:**

- **Dr. Samantha Lee:** Confident Asian female surgeon in scrubs, poised in a modern operating room, highlighting her experience and leadership.

- **Nurse Javier Torres:** Warm Hispanic male nurse, nurturing a patient in a vibrant ward, symbolizing care and empathy.

- **Patient Lydia Bennett:** Bright-eyed Caucasian girl, engaging with medical staff in a cheerful children's ward, fostering player investment.

- **Janitor Malik:** Diligent African American janitor, tidying a spotless corridor, underscoring hygiene's role in healthcare.

- **Resource Manager Ellen:** Focused Caucasian woman, overseeing inventory in a packed storeroom, strategically distributing supplies.

- **Resident Dr. Arun Patel:** Ambitious South Asian male, learning in a dynamic ER, showcasing potential for growth and transformation.

- **Environment:** Lively hospital setting with active wards, dedicated staff, bustling visitor areas, and a state-of-the-art pharmacy room, emphasizing the immersive hospital operations experience.
