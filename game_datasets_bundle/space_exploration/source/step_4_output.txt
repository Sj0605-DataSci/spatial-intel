Summary:

**Game Outline:** Celestial Journey is a strategic, grid-based space exploration game. Players command a spacecraft, discovering cosmic wonders, managing resources, navigating treacherous terrains, and building relations with alien species to achieve mission milestones.

**Characters:**

1. **Commander Vega:** Provides leadership and decision-making, crucial for morale and mission strategy.
2. **Navigator Orion:** Essential for route planning and safe navigation through cosmic threats.
3. **Engineer Lyra:** Maintains ship functionality, crucial for surviving space hazards.
4. **Scientist Nova:** Conducts research on collected artefacts and new phenomena.
5. **Diplomat Solara:** Negotiates trade and alliances with alien species.

**Game States:**

1. **Mission Progress:** Reflects objective completion.
2. **Crew Morale:** Influences team efficiency and decision impact.
3. **Resource Inventory:** Vital for trade and research.
4. **Spacecraft Integrity:** Ensures ship’s survival through challenges.
5. **Alien Relations:** Dictates diplomatic and trade success.
6. **Cosmic Discoveries:** Advances narrative through exploration feats.

**Rules:**

1. Navigate to complete objectives and earn mission progress.
2. Avoid obstacles to maintain spacecraft integrity.
3. Collect resources for upgrades and research developments.
4. Balance decisions to sustain high crew morale.
5. Foster alien relations for alliances and access to resources.
6. Discover phenomena to unlock new missions and storylines.

**Additional Mechanics:**

1. **Zero Gravity Maneuvers:** Simulate space conditions for added realism.
2. **Spacewalks:** Engage players in external repairs and resource collection with a first-person view, enhancing immersion.
3. **Anomalies Exploration:** Encounter unpredictable events, instilling the wonder and fear of space.
