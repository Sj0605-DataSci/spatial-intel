Title: Celestial Journey

Overview: Players guide a team of astronauts on a groundbreaking space exploration mission. Navigate a spacecraft on a grid through asteroid belts and cosmic anomalies, using navigation mechanics to chart new territories. Collect alien artifacts and trade resources across galaxies, fostering interstellar alliances. Encounter alien civilizations and engage in diplomatic quests, unraveling universal mysteries.

Emphasize astronaut experiences—deal with isolation, make critical decisions, and celebrate the awe of discovery. Face emotional stakes like saving endangered planets or prioritizing crew welfare over mission goals. Feel the wonder of the universe through each personal triumph and challenge.


--- STEP 1 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Characters:

1. Commander Vega – The experienced leader guiding the exploration mission. Makes critical decisions, balancing mission objectives and crew welfare.

2. Navigator Orion – Skilled in charting courses through dangerous territories. Provides strategic insights and routes for safe passage.

3. Engineer Lyra – Keeps the spacecraft operational, solving technical challenges. Ensures vital systems are maintained during anomalies.

4. Scientist Nova – Specializes in alien artefact analysis and cosmic phenomena. Drives research, uncovering universal mysteries and fostering cultural exchanges.

5. Diplomat Solara – Manages interactions with alien civilizations. Builds alliances, handling cross-cultural negotiations and establishing trust.

6. Alien Allies – Unique species encountered, each offering distinct resources or quests. Facilitate trade or pose challenges, enriching the narrative.

Environment: The game unfolds amidst dynamic cosmic settings, including vibrant galaxies, mysterious wormholes, and thriving alien worlds, each representing the vastness and wonder of space.


--- STEP 2 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Game States:

1. Mission Progress: Measures objectives completed, including artefact collection and alliances formed. Reflects exploration success.

2. Crew Morale: Represents the emotional state of the team; affected by decisions and events. Critical for maintaining team efficiency.

3. Resource Inventory: Tracks collected resources and artefacts. Essential for trade and research activities.

4. Spacecraft Integrity: Indicates health of the ship, influenced by obstacles encountered. Requires management by Engineer Lyra.

5. Alien Relations: Gauges diplomatic success and trust levels with each civilization. Managed by Diplomat Solara.

6. Cosmic Discoveries: Chronicles new cosmic phenomena uncovered by Scientist Nova, advancing storyline and gameplay.


--- STEP 3 FINAL OUTPUT ---
Rules:

1. Objective Completion: Complete missions to increase Mission Progress. Requires resource collection and alliance formation.

2. Navigation and Obstacles: Use Navigator Orion's skills to move the spacecraft. Avoid collisions to maintain Spacecraft Integrity.

3. Resource Management: Collect resources to enhance Resource Inventory. Use for trade and scientific study.

4. Crew Morale: Make balanced decisions to maintain high Crew Morale. High morale boosts efficiency and success rates.

5. Diplomatic Engagements: Successful interactions with aliens improve Alien Relations. Solara’s diplomacy influences trade options and alliances.

6. Discovery Logging: Uncover new phenomena to update Cosmic Discoveries. These drive narrative progression and new mission availability.

End Goal: Advance civilization through exploration milestones, achieving a breakthrough that concludes the game.
