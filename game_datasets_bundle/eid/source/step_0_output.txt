**Game Title:** Eid Harmony

**Genre:** Puzzle/Hidden Object

**Overview:**
Step into a vibrant Eid celebration on a grid-based scene, centered around a family's journey of gratitude and forgiveness. Arrange intricate family photos and well-wishes in a puzzle to symbolize reconciliation and unity. Uncover hidden letters of gratitude and heartfelt messages between family members, emphasizing emotional connections. Conduct challenges emphasizing acts of kindness and sharing, such as organizing a charitable event or setting up a communal prayer area. Engage in narrative-driven quests that revolve around mending relationships and expressing thankfulness, bringing the spirit of Eid to life through meaningful, unifying interactions.
