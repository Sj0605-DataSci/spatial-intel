**Characters for Eid Harmony:**

1. **Ayaan** - The optimistic family elder leading the reconciliation efforts, guiding players through challenges related to unity and gratitude.

2. **Amira** - A compassionate teenager, focused on organizing charity events, representing the spirit of giving during Eid.

3. **Layla** - The problem-solver, tasked with navigating puzzles related to setting up the family feast, highlighting sharing and community.

4. **Zara** - The mediator, finding hidden letters and messages to mend relationships and promote forgiveness among family members.

5. **Family Members** - Various relatives who provide mini-challenges and insights, each contributing to themes of gratitude and togetherness with personal backstories.

**Environment:** A bustling family home adorned with festive decorations, featuring gathering areas, a dining space, and an outdoor courtyard reflecting Eid's celebratory spirit.
