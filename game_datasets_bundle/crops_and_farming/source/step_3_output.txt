Rules for "Heartland Harvest":

1. Rearrange Crop Tiles: Players must smartly place tiles to optimize Crop Arrangement, boosting productivity.

2. Manage Resources: Maintain Soil Health and Irrigation Levels, aligning farming practices with sustainable goals for higher yields.

3. Adapt to Seasons: Adjust strategy based on the Seasonal Cycle, using Lily's forecasts to prepare for weather-related challenges.

4. Gain Wisdom: Interact with Grandpa Sam to earn Wisdom Points, enhancing decision-making and unlocking advanced techniques.

5. Promote Eco-Practices: Implement Emma's sustainable methods to improve the Sustainability Score, rewarding environmentally friendly actions.

6. Collaborate with Neighbors: Exchange resources to increase the Community Cooperation score, emphasizing teamwork for mutual benefits.

7. Discover Hidden Objects: Use Mentor Bird’s hints to track and find vital items, advancing farm development and strategic options.

8. Victory Condition: Achieve optimum crop yield, high Sustainability Score, and maximum Community Cooperation for a fulfilling farm life experience.
