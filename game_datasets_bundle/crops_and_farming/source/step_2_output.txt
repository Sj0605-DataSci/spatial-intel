State Variables for "Heartland Harvest":

1. Crop Arrangement: Tracks the tile placement on the grid, impacting farm productivity and puzzle completion.

2. Soil Health: Monitors fertility levels, influencing crop yield and requiring strategic decisions by Farmer Ella.

3. Irrigation Levels: Reflects water resources, affecting crop growth and representing Ben’s water scarcity challenges.

4. Seasonal Cycle: Changes with time, altering gameplay dynamics and requiring planning for upcoming weather forecasts.

5. Wisdom Points: Earned by gleaning insights from Grandpa Sam, unlocking tips and enhancing farm management skills.

6. Sustainability Score: Measures eco-practices promoted by Emma, affecting overall farm efficiency and environmental impact.

7. Community Cooperation: Represents interactions with neighbors, boosting resource sharing and collaboration, emphasizing community strength.

8. Hidden Object Tracker: Monitors discovered items with Mentor Bird’s guidance, aiding strategic progress and unlocking new opportunities.
